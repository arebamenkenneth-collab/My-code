From a child's first day to the last day of primary school, learning is hands-on, curious and never rushed.

Ages 6 to 11

Questions parents ask

How do I enrol my child?

Start with a school visit. After your tour we share the admission form, required documents and available places for your child's age group.

What ages do you take?

Children from 2 years old in Early Years through to the end of Primary at about 11 years old.

Can my child join a trial class?

Yes. Choose "Trial class day" when you book and your child will spend a morning with a class, with a teacher looking after them the whole time.

Do you offer after-school clubs?

Yes. Clubs run after lessons and include coding, choir, art, football and chess. Ask about the current list on your visit.
